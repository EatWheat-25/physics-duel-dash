Steps
Step 1: Initial velocity 1 mark · 15s
Taking towards the wall as positive, what is the initial velocity \(u\)?

  2. \(0\)
  3. \(-8.0\,\mathrm{m\,s^{-1}}\)
  4. \(-10\,\mathrm{m\,s^{-1}}\)

Step 3: Change in momentum 1 mark · 15s
What is the change in momentum \(\Delta p\)?
  1. \(-0.80\,\mathrm{N\,s}\)
  2. \(-7.2\,\mathrm{N\,s}\)
  3. \(+0.80\,\mathrm{N\,s}\)
