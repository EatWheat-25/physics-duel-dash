1. SI base units of kinetic energy

Physical Quantities · easy · 3 marks
Main question · 120s

The kinetic energy \(E_{k}\) of an object of mass \(m\) moving at speed \(v\) is given by \[ E_{k}=\tfrac{1}{2}mv^{2} \] Determine the SI base units of kinetic energy.

Steps
Step 1: Unit of mass 1 mark · 15s
In your working, what is the SI base unit of \(m\)?
  1. \(\mathrm{g}\)
  2. \(\mathrm{N}\)
  3. \(\mathrm{kg\,m^{-1}}\)
  4. \(\mathrm{kg}\)
Mass is a base quantity measured in kilograms.
Step 2: Units of v² 1 mark · 15s
What are the SI base units of \(v^{2}\)?
  1. \(\mathrm{m\,s^{-1}}\)
  2. \(\mathrm{m^{2}\,s^{-1}}\)
  3. \(\mathrm{m^{2}\,s^{-2}}\)
  4. \(\mathrm{m\,s^{-2}}\)
\((\mathrm{m\,s^{-1}})^{2}=\mathrm{m^{2}\,s^{-2}}\).
Step 3: Final answer 1 mark · 25s
What are the SI base units of \(E_{k}\)?
  1. \(\mathrm{kg\,m\,s^{-2}}\)
  2. \(\mathrm{kg\,m^{2}\,s^{-2}}\)
  3. \(\mathrm{kg\,m^{2}\,s^{-1}}\)
  4. \(\mathrm{J}\)
\(\tfrac12\) has no units, so \(\mathrm{kg}\times\mathrm{m^{2}\,s^{-2}}\). The joule is a derived unit.

2. Homogeneity: period of a simple pendulum

Physical Quantities · medium · 3 marks
Main question · 150s

The period \(T\) of a simple pendulum of length \(l\) is given by \[ T=2\pi\sqrt{\frac{l}{g}} \] where \(g\) is the acceleration of free fall.

Use SI base units to show whether this equation is homogeneous.

Steps
Step 1: Units of l/g 1 mark · 25s
In your working, what are the SI base units of \(\dfrac{l}{g}\)?
  1. \(\mathrm{s^{2}}\)
  2. \(\mathrm{s^{-2}}\)
  3. \(\mathrm{m^{2}\,s^{-2}}\)
  4. \(\mathrm{s}\)
\(\dfrac{\mathrm{m}}{\mathrm{m\,s^{-2}}}=\mathrm{s^{2}}\).
Step 2: Units of the right-hand side 1 mark · 25s
What are the SI base units of \(2\pi\sqrt{\dfrac{l}{g}}\)?
  1. \(\mathrm{s^{2}}\)
  2. \(\mathrm{s^{-1}}\)
  3. \(\mathrm{m\,s^{-1}}\)
  4. \(\mathrm{s}\)
\(2\pi\) has no units and \(\sqrt{\mathrm{s^{2}}}=\mathrm{s}\).
Step 3: Final answer 1 mark · 25s
What is your conclusion?
  1. Not homogeneous: the right-hand side has units of s²
  2. Not homogeneous: 2π has units
  3. Homogeneous: both sides have units of s
  4. Homogeneous: both sides have units of s⁻¹
\(T\) is a time, in \(\mathrm{s}\), and the right-hand side is also in \(\mathrm{s}\), so the equation is homogeneous.

3. SI base units of a drag constant

Physical Quantities · medium · 3 marks
Main question · 150s

At high speed, the drag force \(F\) on a car is given by \[ F=kv^{2} \] where \(v\) is the speed of the car and \(k\) is a constant.

Determine the SI base units of \(k\).

Steps
Step 1: Rearranged equation 1 mark · 15s
What expression for \(k\) did you use?
  1. \(k=\dfrac{F}{v^{2}}\)
  2. \(k=Fv^{2}\)
  3. \(k=\dfrac{v^{2}}{F}\)
  4. \(k=\dfrac{F}{v}\)
Divide both sides by \(v^{2}\).
Step 2: Units of F 1 mark · 15s
What are the SI base units of \(F\)?
  1. \(\mathrm{kg\,m\,s^{-1}}\)
  2. \(\mathrm{kg\,m\,s^{-2}}\)
  3. \(\mathrm{kg\,m^{2}\,s^{-2}}\)
  4. \(\mathrm{N}\)
\(F=ma\): \(\mathrm{kg\,m\,s^{-2}}\).
Step 3: Final answer 1 mark · 25s
What are the SI base units of \(k\)?
  1. \(\mathrm{kg\,m^{3}\,s^{-4}}\)
  2. \(\mathrm{kg\,s^{-1}}\)
  3. \(\mathrm{kg\,m^{-1}\,s^{-4}}\)
  4. \(\mathrm{kg\,m^{-1}}\)
\(\dfrac{\mathrm{kg\,m\,s^{-2}}}{\mathrm{m^{2}\,s^{-2}}}=\mathrm{kg\,m^{-1}}\).

4. SI base units of resistivity

Determine the SI base units of \(\rho\).

Steps

  2. \(\mathrm{kg\,m^{2}\,s^{-3}\,A^{-1}}\)
  3. \(\mathrm{kg\,m^{2}\,s^{-3}\,A}\)
  4. \(\mathrm{kg\,m\,s^{-3}\,A^{-1}}\)
\(V=\dfrac{P}{I}=\dfrac{\mathrm{kg\,m^{2}\,s^{-3}}}{\mathrm{A}}\).

  1. \(\mathrm{kg\,m^{2}\,s^{-3}\,A^{-1}}\)
  2. \(\mathrm{kg\,m^{2}\,s^{-3}}\)
  3. \(\mathrm{kg\,m^{2}\,s^{-3}\,A^{-2}}\)
  4. \(\mathrm{kg\,m^{2}\,s^{-2}\,A^{-2}}\)

  2. \(\mathrm{m^{2}}\)
  3. \(\mathrm{m^{3}}\)

  2. \(\mathrm{kg\,m^{3}\,s^{-3}\,A^{-1}}\)
  3. \(\mathrm{kg\,m^{2}\,s^{-3}\,A^{-2}}\)
  4. \(\mathrm{kg\,m^{3}\,s^{-3}\,A^{-2}}\)
\(\mathrm{kg\,m^{2}\,s^{-3}\,A^{-2}}\times\mathrm{m}=\mathrm{kg\,m^{3}\,s^{-3}\,A^{-2}}\).
